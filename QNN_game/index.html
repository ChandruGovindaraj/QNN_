    04

    Rx, Ry, Rz on the Bloch Sphere

    Three rotation knobs, one quantum globe. Each knob does something different.

    Ry — The Volume Fader

    Tilts the arrow from north pole |0⟩ toward the equator (50/50 superposition). Controls probability without affecting phase.

    North ↕ South

    Rx — The Sideways Tilt

    Tilts sideways — changes both probability and phase simultaneously.

    ↔ Sideways

    Rz — The Phase Spinner

    Spins around the north-south axis — pure phase shift, no probability change.

    ↻ Spin

    For a full QNN, we need volume (probability) and phase (interference) to sculpt the quantum cloud.

    The Key Insight

    The circuit is like a blindfolded postman sorting letters by zip code. He gets a tip after delivery — and learns which addresses give big tips purely by experience.

    After many deliveries, the rotation angles have sorted the highest-tip addresses to the top — without ever reading what's inside the envelope.
